# Phase 10b: boost Home

The owner ran Phases 9 and 10 and found the app "about the same other than the orb moving". They chose to make Home look like the Living glass samples before building the other screens.

## Rule changes the owner accepted

- A full-screen glow is now allowed on Home and the lock moment (the tokens said never a screen-wide wash).
- Glass controls carry a light rim (the rules said no borders on cards).
- Both apply only with the Living glass switch on.

## What changes (switch on only)

| Piece | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Orb | Stronger glass: darker edge, flowing light inside, lens refraction, reflection crescent, tinted rim, sharper highlight, larger response to tilt. |
| Tap | Tapping the orb ripples across and outside the glass, bounces it and ticks the haptic. The locked orb still starts a scan. |
| Background | A breathing, drifting glow fills Home: slate unlocked, ember locked, crossfading between them. Still when motion is limited. |
| Lock moment | An ember bloom spreads from the orb across the screen and settles into the locked background. |
| Controls | Mode pill, settings button, intention field, chips, Timer and tab bar become frosted glass. Scan gets a gloss and glow. The tab selection slides and glows. |

With the switch off, and in Release, nothing changes. No logic changes. Other screens are untouched, apart from the shared tab bar.
