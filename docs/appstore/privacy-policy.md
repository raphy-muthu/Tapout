# Tapout privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13

Tapout does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any data about you. This page explains what that means in practice.

## What stays on your iPhone

Everything Tapout knows lives on your iPhone and nowhere else: your modes, places, schedules, lock history, interests and settings. It is kept in storage shared between Tapout and its own extensions (an App Group). Your emergency pass count is kept in the iOS Keychain, so it survives reinstalling the app.

Tapout has no account, no login and no servers. Nothing is uploaded.

## Screen Time

Tapout uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to seal the apps you choose. iOS handles your selection and the blocking itself. Tapout never sees which apps you use, how often, or for how long. The selection you make is an opaque token that only iOS can read.

## NFC

Tapout reads an NFC tag only to check its ID, so it can tell your paired tags from other tags. It reads nothing else from a tag and writes nothing to it.

## Analytics, ads and tracking

Tapout has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code that collects data, and no tracking.

## The weekly recap

The recap image is built on your iPhone. It leaves your phone only if you choose to save or share it.

## Children

Tapout collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will carry a new effective date.
