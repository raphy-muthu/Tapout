## Description (4000, 1601 used)

Tapout puts your distracting apps away with one tap.

Stick an NFC tag somewhere you choose, like the kitchen counter or your desk. Pair it once. Tap the tag and the apps you picked are sealed. Tap it again to open them. The tag is the way back in. Passes are for emergencies.

How it works
- Pair tags to places. Each tag is a named place: Kitchen, Desk, Bedside.
- Tap to lock. Your chosen apps show a calm Sealed screen.
- Tap again to unlock. Optionally write a short intention before you start.

Modes
Make a mode for each part of your day. Each mode has its own apps, an optional schedule, and the places that can unlock it. Always-allowed apps are set once in Settings.

Schedules
A scheduled mode locks at its start time, even if Tapout is closed. A schedule never unlocks for you. Only a tag does.

Five emergency passes, for life
You get five emergency passes in total, ever. A pass opens your apps for five minutes, then they seal again. Passes never refill.

Insights and the weekly recap
See how long you have kept your phone down, day by day. The weekly recap is a picture you can save or share.

Live Activity and widgets
A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island shows the lock timer. Widgets show whether you are locked and your time today.

Private by design
Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Tapout never sees which apps you use.

You will need NFC tags, sold separately online. Any NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 sticker works. Tapout needs an iPhone that runs iOS 17 with NFC (iPhone XS or later) and Screen Time access.

## App Privacy

Data Not Collected.

Why: Tapout has no servers and no account. Everything is stored on the device in App Group storage and the Keychain (the emergency pass count). It uses no analytics or advertising SDKs. Screen Time selections are opaque tokens handled by iOS.

## App Review notes

Tapout needs a physical NFC tag to lock and unlock, and reviewers will not have one. A screen recording of the full flow (pair a tag, lock, a blocked app showing the Sealed screen, unlock, an emergency pass) is attached to this submission. **OWNER: record and attach.**

Family Controls: Tapout uses the Family Controls entitlement to let the user choose apps with Apple's FamilyActivityPicker, and ManagedSettings to shield those apps while locked. The Sealed screen comes from a ManagedSettingsUI shield extension, and DeviceActivity runs the user's optional schedules. Tapout never reads which apps the user opens. The selection is an opaque token.

There is no login and no account.

To review the app without a tag:
1. Open Tapout and follow onboarding.
2. Allow Screen Time.
3. Choose apps, then pick an optional schedule.
4. At "Pair your first tag", tap Not now.
5. You land on Home, unlocked. Modes (including the app picker and schedules), Places, Insights and Settings all work.
6. Locking, the Sealed screen and emergency passes need a paired NFC tag. They are shown in the attached recording.

Bank, transit and hotel cards are not read. An NTAG213/215/216 sticker is needed.
